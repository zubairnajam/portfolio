01About

A bit about me.

I'm a Computer Science student at FAST-NUCES (National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences) and I graduate in 2027. Most of what I do is building software and AI projects, and I care a lot about security too, so I enjoy it most when the two overlap.

On the building side, I've made a chatbot that answers questions about any YouTube video, a camera that takes a photo when you hold up your fingers, and an online Mafia game you can play with friends. I also test mobile apps part time, which has made me pretty good at spotting the ways things go wrong.

On the security side, I spent two months as a SOC intern going through SIEM alerts and working out which ones were real. At home I run a Wazuh lab with an Ubuntu server and a Windows machine, and I practice writing Splunk searches that catch things like brute force logins. I have the Google Cybersecurity certificate and I've finished TryHackMe's SOC Level 1 path.
